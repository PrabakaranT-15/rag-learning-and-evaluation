# Traditional Napa Cabbage Kimchi

## Ingredients

Percentages here are calculated against the 2000 g of prepared napa cabbage rather than against flour, since this is a vegetable ferment rather than a dough.

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of cabbage |
|---|---|---|
| Napa cabbage | 2000 g | 100% |
| Coarse sea salt (for brining) | 160 g | 8% |
| Gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes) | 100 g | 5% |
| Korean radish, julienned | 400 g | 20% |
| Garlic, minced | 60 g | 3% |
| Ginger, grated | 20 g | 1% |
| Saeujeot (salted fermented shrimp) | 60 g | 3% |
| Anchovy fish sauce | 60 g | 3% |
| Glutinous rice flour | 30 g | 1.5% |
| Caster sugar | 20 g | 1% |

Total yield is approximately 2.9 kg of finished kimchi.

## Method

Quarter the cabbage lengthwise keeping the core attached, then brine it using the full 160 g of coarse sea salt, working the salt between the leaves and concentrating it on the thick white stems. Leave for 4 to 6 hours, turning every 2 hours.

The cabbage is correctly brined when a thick white stem bends into a U shape without snapping. This bend test matters more than the clock, because cabbage size and moisture content vary considerably between heads.

Rinse three times in cold water and drain cut side down for at least one hour. Residual water dilutes the paste and can spoil the ferment.

Make the rice porridge by whisking the glutinous rice flour into 250 ml of cold water, heating gently for 5 minutes until it thickens, then stirring in the sugar and cooling completely. The porridge feeds the lactobacillus and speeds the early fermentation.

Combine the cooled porridge with the gochugaru and let it stand for 10 minutes so the chilli flakes bloom, then mix in the garlic, ginger, saeujeot and anchovy fish sauce. Fold through the julienned radish.

Wearing gloves, spread the paste over every leaf, working from the stem end outward. Pack into a jar, pressing down firmly to eliminate air pockets, and leave 5 cm of headspace.

Ferment at 20C for 2 days until bubbles appear and the smell turns sour, then transfer to the refrigerator at 4C. The kimchi is edible immediately but reaches its best flavour after 2 weeks.

## Allergen Note

Contains fish in the form of anchovy fish sauce and contains crustacean shellfish in the form of salted fermented shrimp. Not suitable for vegetarians or vegans. Contains no dairy, no egg, no nuts and no gluten. Despite its name, glutinous rice flour contains no gluten.
