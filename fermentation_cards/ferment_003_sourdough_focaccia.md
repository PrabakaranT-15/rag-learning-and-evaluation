# Sourdough Focaccia

## Ingredients

Baker's percentages are calculated against the 1000 g of strong white flour. Final hydration is 85%.

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Strong white bread flour | 1000 g | 100% |
| Water (24C) | 850 g | 85% |
| Rye levain (100% hydration) | 250 g | 25% |
| Fine sea salt | 22 g | 2.2% |
| Extra virgin olive oil | 60 g | 6% |

Total dough weight is 2182 g, which fills one 30 x 40 cm tray.

## Method

Mix the flour and 800 g of the water until no dry flour remains and rest for 45 minutes. Reserve the last 50 g of water for the salt.

Add the levain and mix through, then rest 30 minutes. Add the fine sea salt with the reserved water and squeeze until fully incorporated. Finally drizzle in the olive oil and work it in. The dough will look broken and greasy at first and will come back together after two minutes of folding.

Bulk ferment for 3 to 4 hours at 24C with four sets of coil folds spaced 30 minutes apart. At 85% hydration the dough is slack, so use a wet hand and lift rather than stretch.

Oil the tray generously, tip the dough in without deflating it, and spread it gently towards the corners. If it springs back, wait 20 minutes and try again rather than forcing it. Cold retard in the refrigerator for 12 hours.

Bring the tray to room temperature for 90 minutes before baking. Dimple the surface firmly with oiled fingertips, pressing all the way down to the tray so the dough does not dome, then drizzle with a further tablespoon of olive oil and scatter with flaky salt.

Bake at 240C for 22 minutes until the top is deep golden and the underside is crisp when lifted with a spatula. Lift the focaccia straight out of the tray onto a rack so the base does not steam.

## Allergen Note

Contains wheat and rye, therefore contains gluten. Contains no dairy, no egg and no nuts. Suitable for vegans.
