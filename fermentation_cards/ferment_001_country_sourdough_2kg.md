# 2kg Country Sourdough Loaf

## Ingredients

Total flour weight is 2000 g and all baker's percentages below are calculated against that total flour weight. Final hydration is 80%.

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Strong white bread flour | 1700 g | 85% |
| Wholemeal flour | 300 g | 15% |
| Water (28C) | 1600 g | 80% |
| Fine sea salt | 40 g | 2% |
| Rye levain (100% hydration) | 400 g | 20% |

Total dough weight is 4040 g, which divides into two loaves of roughly 2020 g each.

## Method

Autolyse first. Combine the strong white flour, the wholemeal flour and 1500 g of the water in a large tub, mixing until no dry flour remains, then cover and rest for 60 minutes. Hold back the remaining 100 g of water for the salt stage.

Add the rye levain and squeeze it through the dough until fully dispersed. Rest for 30 minutes.

Now add the fine sea salt together with the reserved 100 g of water. The salt goes in after the autolyse and after the levain, never at the start, because salt tightens the gluten network and slows the enzymatic activity the autolyse is meant to encourage. Squeeze and fold until the dough feels smooth and no grittiness remains at the base of the tub.

Bulk ferment for 4 to 5 hours at 24C, performing a set of coil folds every 45 minutes for the first three hours. The bulk is finished when the dough has risen by roughly 50%, feels aerated, and shows a domed surface with visible bubbles at the edge of the tub.

Divide into two pieces, pre-shape lightly and bench rest for 25 minutes. Shape into boules, place seam side up in floured bannetons, and cold retard in the refrigerator at 4C for 12 to 16 hours.

Bake straight from the refrigerator in a preheated cast iron combo cooker. Bake at 250C for 20 minutes with the lid on, then remove the lid, drop the oven to 230C and bake for a further 25 minutes until the crust is deep brown and the internal temperature reaches 96C.

Cool on a rack for at least 2 hours before cutting.

## Allergen Note

Contains wheat and rye, therefore contains gluten. Contains no dairy, no egg and no nuts. Suitable for vegans. Produced in a bakery that also handles milk, egg and sesame.
