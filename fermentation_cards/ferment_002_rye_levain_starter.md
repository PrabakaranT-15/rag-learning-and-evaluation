# Rye Levain Starter

## Ingredients

This is a 100% hydration levain, meaning the water weight equals the flour weight. Baker's percentages are calculated against the 100 g of rye flour.

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Wholegrain rye flour | 100 g | 100% |
| Water (26C) | 100 g | 100% |
| Mature sourdough starter | 20 g | 20% |

Total levain yield is 220 g, which is enough to build the 400 g of levain required by the 2kg Country Sourdough after one further refreshment.

## Method

Weigh the mature starter into a clear straight-sided jar and mark the level with an elastic band so the rise can be judged by eye rather than by guesswork.

Add the water at 26C and stir until the starter is dispersed and the liquid turns cloudy. Add the wholegrain rye flour and mix to a thick paste. Rye holds far more water than white flour, so at 100% hydration this will look like a stiff batter rather than a dough.

Cover loosely and ferment for 8 to 12 hours at 22 to 24C. The levain is ripe when it has at least doubled from the elastic band mark, the surface is domed and covered in fine bubbles, and it smells sharply sour with a faint acetone note. A levain that has risen and then collapsed back to a flat, watery surface has gone past ripe and will give a slack, over-acidified dough.

The float test is a useful confirmation: drop a teaspoon of ripe levain into room temperature water and it should float rather than sink.

Refresh at least once every 24 hours if the starter is kept at room temperature, or once a week if it is stored at 4C in the refrigerator.

## Allergen Note

Contains rye, therefore contains gluten. Contains no dairy, no egg and no nuts. Suitable for vegans.
