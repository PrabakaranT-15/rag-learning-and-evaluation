# Sourdough Brioche

## Ingredients

Baker's percentages are calculated against the 500 g of strong white flour. This is an enriched dough, so the fat and egg percentages are high.

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Strong white bread flour | 500 g | 100% |
| Whole milk (cold) | 150 g | 30% |
| Eggs (about 4 medium) | 200 g | 40% |
| Unsalted butter (softened) | 200 g | 40% |
| Caster sugar | 60 g | 12% |
| Rye levain (100% hydration) | 100 g | 20% |
| Fine sea salt | 10 g | 2% |

Total dough weight is 1220 g, which makes two loaves in 900 g tins.

## Method

Mix the flour, cold milk, eggs, sugar and levain in a stand mixer with the dough hook on low for 4 minutes until a shaggy dough forms. Add the fine sea salt and mix on medium for a further 8 minutes until the dough is smooth and pulls cleanly from the sides of the bowl.

Only now begin adding the softened butter, roughly 50 g at a time, waiting until each addition has fully disappeared before adding the next. The butter goes in last and gradually, because adding it at the start coats the flour in fat and prevents the gluten network from developing at all. Expect this stage to take 10 to 12 minutes.

The dough is ready when it passes the windowpane test: a small piece stretched thin should become translucent without tearing.

Bulk ferment for 5 to 6 hours at 22C, then cold retard for 14 to 18 hours at 4C. Enriched sourdough is very difficult to shape at room temperature, so the cold retard is a handling step as much as a flavour step.

Divide, shape into four balls per tin, and prove for 4 to 5 hours at 24C until the dough reaches the rim of the tin and springs back slowly when pressed.

Egg wash and bake at 180C for 30 minutes until deep golden and the internal temperature reaches 92C. Turn out of the tins immediately to stop the sides collapsing.

## Allergen Note

Contains wheat and rye, therefore contains gluten. Contains milk and butter, therefore contains dairy. Contains egg, both in the dough and in the egg wash. Contains no nuts. Suitable for vegetarians but not for vegans.
